# Vision

## North star

One sentence: where does this product want to be in 12–18 months, and for whom?

## Core thesis

What do we believe about the world that, if true, makes this product the right thing to build?

## Wedge

What is the smallest, sharpest piece of value we can ship today that is *uniquely* this product — not the generic version of the category?

## Distribution

How does this product reach its first 100 / 1,000 / 10,000 users? What channel is load-bearing?

## Phases

Rough sketch of the phase plan — milestone names and what each unlocks.

- **Phase 0 — foundation:** what has to be true before users can install.
- **Phase 1 — wedge:** the differentiating feature.
- **Phase 2 — broaden:** expand from the wedge.

## What we are NOT

The adjacent products we are explicitly not competing with — the boundaries that keep scope honest.
